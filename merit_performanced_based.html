🎓 Merit / Merit-Performance Based Scholarships

(सरकारी + निजी छात्रवृत्तियाँ – Academic Performance आधारित)

Merit-Based Scholarship वह छात्रवृत्ति है जो छात्रों को उनके Academic Performance (Marks, CGPA, Entrance Score) के आधार पर दी जाती है। कुछ योजनाएँ Merit + Income दोनों पर आधारित होती हैं।

🟢 1️⃣ National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS)

  • Class 9–12 के छात्रों के लिए
  • ₹12,000 प्रति वर्ष (₹1,000 प्रति माह)
  • Class 8 में चयन परीक्षा के आधार पर
  • Minimum 55% (SC/ST/PwD के लिए 50%)
  • Family income ≤ ₹3.5 लाख
  • Apply: National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

🟢 2️⃣ Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (Minority)

  • ₹20,000 तक fee reimbursement
  • Hosteller: ₹1000/माह (10 महीने)
  • Day Scholar: ₹500/माह (10 महीने)
  • ≥ 50% marks required
  • Income ≤ ₹2.5 लाख
  • Minority Community (Muslim, Sikh, Christian, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi)
  • Apply: NSP / State Portal

🟢 3️⃣ State Merit Scholarships

  • 10वीं / 12वीं में high % लाने वाले छात्रों के लिए
  • ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 या अधिक (राज्य अनुसार)
  • Apply: State Education Portal

🟡 4️⃣ Board / School Merit Scholarships

CBSE Single Girl Child Merit Scholarship

  • Class 10 पास लड़कियों के लिए
  • Class 11 में enrolled होना चाहिए
  • Apply: CBSE Official Website

🟣 5️⃣ College / University Merit Scholarships

Example: BITS Pilani Merit Scholarship

  • Top 1% → 100% Tuition Fee Waiver
  • Next 2% → 40% Fee Waiver

🔵 6️⃣ Private / Corporate Merit Scholarships

  • HDFC Bank Parivartan Scholarship (₹30,000 – ₹75,000)
  • SBI Asha Scholarship (Top Institutes students)
  • Merit + Need based programs

📊 7️⃣ Typical Eligibility Criteria

Parameter Requirement
Academic Performance High % / CGPA / Merit List
Income Limit Scheme dependent
Institution Recognised School/College
Duration 1 Year (Renewable)

📋 8️⃣ Required Documents

  • Marksheet / Merit Proof
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Details
  • Admission Proof

📝 9️⃣ Apply Process

  • National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
  • State Govt Portal
  • College Admission Portal
  • Bank/Foundation Website
👉 Apply on NSP
