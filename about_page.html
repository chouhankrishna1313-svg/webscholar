About Scholarship Portal

Welcome to our Scholarship Portal! This platform is designed to help students easily find government and private scholarships based on their category, merit, and eligibility criteria. Our mission is to provide a centralized, easy-to-use resource so that no student misses financial aid opportunities.

This portal lists scholarships for SC, ST, OBC, and other categories, including pre-matric, post-matric, merit-based, and annual aid programs. Users can select their category and instantly see which scholarships they are eligible for.

Our goal is to simplify the scholarship search process and ensure that every deserving student gets the support they need to pursue higher education. We continuously update the portal with the latest scholarship schemes from central and state governments, educational boards, and private institutions.
