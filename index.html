How to Apply for Scholarships in 2026?

Finding the right scholarship is crucial for students from SC, ST, and OBC categories. Our portal simplifies this by categorizing schemes based on government guidelines. Whether you are looking for Pre-Matric or Post-Matric aid, follow these steps:

  • Check Eligibility: Use the dropdown above to see which schemes match your category.
  • Required Documents: Keep your Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, and Marksheets ready.
  • Official Portals: Always apply through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or state-specific websites.

We update our database regularly to ensure you don't miss any deadlines. Make sure to read the terms and conditions of each scholarship before applying.
